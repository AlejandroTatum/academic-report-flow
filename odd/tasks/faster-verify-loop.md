# Faster verify loop

Cut the repeated verifier rounds of the report flow without weakening the independent verification. Measured on the AA3 run of 2026-11-11 (`dbp-aa3-adr-alternativas-arquitectonicas`): 19 min from intake done to validated v1, three verifier rounds (3 min 12 s full, 2 min 9 s full, 1 min 23 s incremental).
Worktree `academic-report-automation-worktrees/verify-loop`, branch `feat/faster-verify-loop` (base main 60b03bc). Push, PR and merge are the user's decisions.

## Specs

S1. Step 1 as proposed to the user: "Generar un PDF especulativo en paralelo con la primera verificación. Hoy la inspección visual ocurre al final [...] El build tarda 49 s; si corre mientras trabaja el verificador, los hallazgos visuales y los semánticos se corrigen en la misma ronda de edición." `tools/build.py` already has no verify gate (L2), so this is flow guidance: the skill docs (`SKILL.md`, `references/production.md` Verify and the generate step, `references/approval.md`) and the `doc_status` guidance for `verify` say that, right after launching the verifier, the executor decides format, typesets, runs `build.py` and inspects every page while the verifier works; that build is a preview, its visual findings join the verifier's findings in ONE edit round, and v1 is the build made after that round. No gate is removed: `doc_status` still orders the phases and v1 is still presented only with verify `done`.

S2. Step 2 as proposed: "Tratar el renombre de un encabezado como edición acotada cuando el texto de la sección no cambió. Las citas de evidencia siguen intactas, así que la herramienta puede arrastrar la verificación sola, como ya hace con ediciones de hasta 40 palabras." Applies to the verification marker only (`content_check._bounded_since_check`): the approval marker and the final review marker keep today's rule (any heading change is unbounded). A heading-only rename keeps `content-check.yml` when: the headings have the same count, order and levels (none added or removed); the changed words, heading words included, are at most 40 in total; every rubric criterion's `section` still resolves (same `section_sha256` keys); the rubric checks still pass on the new body; and every existing condition of the bounded rule still holds (no accepted gaps, `found` quotes present, citations resolve). Reproduction case from the AA3 run: `### Referencias` renamed to `### Fuentes de la decisión`, nothing else changed, forced a verifier round.

S3. Step 3 as proposed: "Chequear los techos de palabras en el body check." Correction after exploring (L2): the body check already enforces `max_words` (`word_budget`, prose words, tables excluded). The real defect is that the verifier does not know it: on AA3 it counted `wc -w` with tables (551 vs 450) and reported a length finding the mechanical check contradicts, and it said "No word-limit check appeared in the tool output". So: the verify brief (`--verify-brief`, full and `--since`) states that length is checked mechanically on prose words with tables excluded and that the verifier must not count words or report length; and the `--verification` output prints the same `word_budget` line the body check prints.

S4. Step 4 as proposed: "Congelar la rúbrica al terminar el plan. [...] cualquier cambio posterior invalida todo lo verificado." Two parts. (a) Rule and message: the skill docs say the rubric is frozen once the draft starts (fix the draft, not the rubric; a rubric change after that needs the user's instruction), and when the verification is stale because `rubric.yml` changed, the `doc_status` verify detail says so explicitly instead of the generic "inputs changed". (b) A change that only touches `max_words` values (top level or per criterion) never changes the requirement matrix, so `--since` must not force a full verify for it: criteria whose section text is unchanged are carried over as with an unchanged rubric. Any other rubric change still forces a full verify. The marker stays bound to the exact `rubric_sha256` (staleness unchanged); the new information needed to recognize a ceilings-only change is recorded in `content-check.yml` going forward, and a marker without it behaves as today.

S5. Step 5 as proposed: "Marcar en el plan los requisitos que el texto no puede cerrar. G26 (ADR versionado en el repo) iba a quedar parcial sí o sí; marcarlo de entrada como 'lo cierra una persona' evita que cada ronda termine en `fail` por lo mismo." `rubric.yml` accepts an optional top-level `needs_person: [{item, reason}]`, validated like `not_applicable` (same shape, no extra keys; an item cannot be in both lists). The verify brief lists those items and tells the verifier to report each as `partial` with the recorded reason as `gap` when the text only states the plan, without spending a finding on it. The `--verification` output and the `doc_status` verify detail label such a `partial` as waiting for a person, apart from fixable findings. Nothing else changes: the criterion stays `falta`, the check verdict stays `fail`, publication is still refused until the person accepts the gap with `accepted_gaps.py`; no tool accepts it on its own.

S6. User request: "dale con los 5 pasos y luego regeneras el mismo doc, y comparas tiempos". After S1-S5 pass, regenerate the AA3 report from zero in a new work folder with the worktree tools, logging the same phase marks as `/tmp/aa3-bench.log`, and report both runs side by side, phase by phase, with the number of verifier rounds. The existing folder `dbp-aa3-adr-alternativas-arquitectonicas` is not modified.
